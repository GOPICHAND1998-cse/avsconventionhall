ABOUT OUR VENUE

Celebrate Life's Beautiful Moments With Us

Welcome to AVS Convention Hall, located in Srinivasa Colony, Mahabubnagar, Telangana.

From weddings and receptions to birthday celebrations, traditional ceremonies, family gatherings and corporate events, we welcome you to celebrate your important occasions with us.

Our facilities include a spacious hall, AC, ample parking, power backup, catering support and customizable decoration.
